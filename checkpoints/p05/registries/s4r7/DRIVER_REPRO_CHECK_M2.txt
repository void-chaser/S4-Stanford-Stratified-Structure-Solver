The driver therefore REPRODUCES the injection.  The remaining disagreement is the judged route:
  - plancall:12:1 is a TWICE point in the historical record (M-twice-fft-plan-12): inj_call = 2 (column 8 of
    RESULTS_R4_PLANCALL_pass1.tsv), rc1 0, rc2 1, class REPORTED_FAILURE.  Judged with inj_call 2, classify() gives

    (the same class of error as E7-8), and the registration must be regenerated before any M2 run (not yet hashed in a run).
